# Wizard multiplayer on the game server: validation run, 2026-09-21

Follow-up to `report-2026-09-20.md`. Same rig (`scripts/netlab/run.mjs` + `shaper.mjs`), same three profiles, same production bundle served by `vite preview`, but the app now plays through the Cloudflare Durable Object game server (`server/`) instead of Firestore. Rows:

- `current`: the Firestore build that was live until 2026-09-20 (from the previous report)
- `slim`: the Firestore build with round archives, live 2026-09-20 to 2026-09-21
- `prototype`: the throwaway DO transport test from the previous report (no game logic, tiny delta messages)
- `server`: the real app on the real game server, what ships now

What the server rows say:

- A play reaches the other phones in about 490 ms at a 400 ms round trip and about 930 ms at 800 ms: one round trip plus rendering. Against the last Firestore build that is 30% faster on slow3g and 54% faster on the awful link, and the worst case on slow3g dropped from 1.3 s to 0.53 s.
- The per-play frame is a full snapshot (3.6 to 4.7 KB of JSON), but the socket compresses it, so the wire cost is about 140 B per play and 113 KB per player for a whole 6-round game, versus 364 KB on the slim Firestore build. On the 6 KB/s uplink of the awful profile this is what keeps the game responsive.
- The cut-off phone in the flaky profile was back and acting 5.6 s after its link returned (Firestore: 8.7 to 23 s). The 12.8 s worst-case propagation in that column is a play that landed while the victim was offline, so it includes the 8 s cut itself.
- Zero plays lost on any profile; the short full game (`server-finish`) ended with the round-by-round table complete, 3 of 3 rounds.

`scripts/netlab/server-check.mjs` (23 protocol checks over raw sockets against the deployed server) passed before these runs.

Three real Chrome sessions per run, 6 rounds of a 3-player game, every connection shaped at the TCP level (bandwidth cap + one-way delay); flaky = slow3g with one player cut off for 8 s every 40 s.
